# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

This project presents an end-to-end **sales performance analysis** built using **Power BI**, focusing on executive insights, product performance, and regional market dynamics.

The dashboard is designed to simulate a real-world business scenario and demonstrate data modeling, DAX calculations, and analytical storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Provide a high-level executive overview of sales performance
- Analyze individual product behavior and growth over time
- Identify regional strengths, sales concentration, and market opportunities
- Apply best practices in data modeling, DAX, and dashboard design

---

## 🧠 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Executive Overview
**Purpose:** High-level business performance monitoring

**Key KPIs**
- Total Sales
- Sales Year-over-Year (YoY)
- Sales YoY Delta
- Sales Growth %

**Visuals**
- Sales Trend Over Time
- Top Products by Sales
- Top States by Sales
- Interactive slicers for Year and Region

📌 *This page is designed for executives and decision-makers.*

---

### 2️⃣ Product Performance
**Purpose:** Deep-dive analysis of an individual product

**Key KPIs**
- Total Sales (Selected Product)
- Sales YoY
- Sales Growth %
- First Sale Year

**Visuals**
- Sales Trend Over Time (Product-level)
- Top States by Sales
- Insight card highlighting product behavior and concentration

📌 *This page explains how a single product performs, where it sells most, and how fast it is growing.*

---

### 3️⃣ Regional / Market Analysis
**Purpose:** Market and geographic performance evaluation

**Key KPIs**
- Total Sales
- Top Region by Sales
- Top State by Sales
- Sales Concentration %

**Visuals**
- Sales by Region
- Sales Trend by Region Over Time
- Region × Year matrix (heatmap-style analysis)
- Regional insight summary

📌 *This page identifies regional dominance, imbalance, and potential growth opportunities.*

---

## ⚙️ Technical Highlights

- Star schema data model (Fact & Dimension tables)
- Advanced DAX measures:
  - Year-over-Year calculations
  - Growth percentages
  - Ranking (Top N)
  - Sales concentration analysis
- Proper filter context handling
- Clean and consistent dashboard design
- Insight-driven storytelling (not just visuals)

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **DAX**
- **Power Query**
